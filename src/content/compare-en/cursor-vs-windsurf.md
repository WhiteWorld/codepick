Cursor and Windsurf are both VS Code forks. The 2026-10-16 pricing pages no longer support "Cursor Pro $20 vs Windsurf Pro $15." This update changes the price conclusions. The product differences stay as they were framed.

## At a glance

| | Cursor | Windsurf |
|---|--------|----------|
| Paid individual tiers | Plus $8 / Premium $30 / Super $100 / Ultra $200 | Pro $20 / Max $200 |
| Free | Free $0, limited AI usage | Free $0, light quota. Credit count not published |
| Models | Plus is Grok only. Third-party models from Premium are API-priced | Pro lists frontier models from OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and SpaceXAI |
| Completion | Tab | Tab + Supercomplete. Free includes unlimited Tab |
| Agent | Agent. Cloud agents are listed on Premium | Cascade. Pro includes Devin Cloud |
| China access score | 4/10 | 3/10 |

Sources: [Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/en/pricing) and [Windsurf / Devin pricing](https://devin.ai/pricing), both checked 2026-10-16.

## Pricing

**Cursor**, individual:

| Plan | Price | What the page says |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| Free | $0 | Limited AI usage |
| Plus | $8/mo | Weekly pool shared with Grok. Grok models only. Dollar size not published |
| Premium | $30/mo | 4x Plus. Includes cloud agents. Third-party models at API list price |
| Super | $100/mo | 15x Plus. Third-party models extra |
| Ultra | $200/mo | 50x Plus. Third-party models extra |
| Teams | From $30/user/mo | The page shows $30+ |

Pro at $20, the $20 credit pool, and unlimited Auto are not on this page.

**Windsurf**, individual:

| Plan | Price | What the page says |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| Free | $0 | Light agent quota, limited models, unlimited inline edits and Tab. It no longer says 25 credits |
| Pro | $20/mo | Higher quotas, frontier models, Devin Cloud. Credit count not published |
| Max | $200/mo | Pro's features with higher quotas. The count is not published |
| Teams | $80/mo plus $40/mo per full seat | Not a flat $40/user |

The 2026-03-19 grandfathered $15 rate and the student discount are not on the current page. Whether they still apply is unknown.

> **Conclusion:** Windsurf is not "$5 a month cheaper." Cursor Plus ($8) costs less than Windsurf Pro ($20), and it is Grok-only. For frontier models, Windsurf Pro is $20 and Cursor Premium is $30, with Cursor's third-party models billed again at API rates. Windsurf does not publish a credit count, so the stickers alone do not say who includes more usage. Unlimited Auto is not a reason to pick Cursor.

## Coding

**Cursor** still lists Claude, GPT, Gemini, and Composer in the tool data. The third-party names are not on Plus. From Premium they are billed at API list price.

**Windsurf** Cascade keeps context across the task. The Pro plan lists frontier models from OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and SpaceXAI, plus Devin Cloud. SWE-1.5 is still in the model list.

> **Conclusion:** A model list is not an included allowance. Cursor's third-party models start at Premium and are extra. Windsurf Pro puts frontier models on the $20 plan, and does not publish the credit count.

## Completion

- **Cursor Tab** is still a product difference. The pricing page does not say whether Tab draws from the weekly pool, and it does not say Tab is unlimited
- **Windsurf** Free states unlimited inline edits and Tab. Supercomplete is still the cross-file completion

> **Conclusion:** For "completion is not metered," Windsurf Free says unlimited Tab. The Cursor pricing page does not.

## Editors

- **Windsurf** also ships JetBrains and Vim / Neovim plugins
- **Cursor** tool data says JetBrains works through ACP. That was not part of this pricing check

> If IntelliJ, PyCharm, or GoLand is the main editor, check the plugin against your version. Do not decide from the sticker alone.

## Which plan

**Cursor Plus**, if:

- You only use Grok models
- You accept a weekly pool whose dollar size is not published

**Cursor Premium or above**, if:

- You want cloud agents, or Claude / GPT / Gemini
- You accept API list price for third-party models. The monthly fee is not a cap

**Windsurf Pro**, if:

- A $20 sticker that lists frontier models and Devin Cloud is what you need
- You can accept an unpublished credit count
- You need the JetBrains or Vim plugin

Do not choose on "save $5" or "unlimited Auto." Both referred to the old prices.

## Summary

- **Cursor Plus** is $8, Grok only, weekly pool, dollar size not published
- **Cursor Premium** is a $30 sticker, with third-party models extra
- **Windsurf Pro** is a $20 sticker, credit count not published, and Free states unlimited Tab

Both have a $0 tier. Try that before paying for a plan that does not include the models you use.

> Prices checked 2026-10-16. Cursor does not publish the pool's dollar size. Windsurf does not publish a credit count, the $15 grandfather rate, or a student discount.
